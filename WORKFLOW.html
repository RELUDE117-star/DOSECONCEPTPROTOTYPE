Application Flow

Three main modes accessible via the D logo menu. Camera runs continuously — QR presence drives what's shown. Touch/tap to dispense.

System

Camera & QR Presence

The camera continuously scans for QR codes. Position in the frame determines slot assignment. Presence drives what's visible across all screens.

Property Value Notes
Scan interval 0.5s Camera captures and decodes every 500ms
Presence timeout 15.0s QR must be re-seen within 15s or pill disappears from view
Position ordering Right → Left = Top → Bottom Rightmost QR in frame = blue (slot 0), leftmost = yellow (slot 3)
QR payload {"med":"Name","slot":"color"} JSON string. Slot field used for identification
Camera resolution 1280 × 720 RGB888 format, Camera Module 3 Wide (IMX708)
Autofocus AfMode=2 Continuous autofocus enabled
Data persistence ~/dose-home-station/med_data.json Name, count, schedule saved permanently after first scan

Mapping Rule

  • pyzbar returns rect.left (x-pixel) for each detected QR
  • Sort all detected QRs by x-position, descending (rightmost first)
  • Assign: index 0 → SLOT_KEYS[0] (blue), index 1 → SLOT_KEYS[1] (red), etc.
  • Maximum 4 QR codes mapped per frame
